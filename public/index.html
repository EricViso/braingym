Why us.

While traditional therapy provides a safe clinical environment, art allows personal expression. We are the ones who merge these two approaches, a ethically-supervised space where you can move and create to express what words cannot convey.

We don’t guess what pain feels like; we know. Our methodology is rooted in the lived experience of our founder, our community members, and refined by the medical expertise of our co-founder; a doctor.

Healing isn’t a one-size-fits-all solution. A corporate executive dealing with burnout and a young person experiencing anxiety need different strategies, which is why we customize our Creative Wellness modules to suit your unique emotional needs.

Frequently Asked Questions.

myWIPhealing stands for ‘my work-in-progress healing’. We are a Malaysian social enterprise dedicated to holistic recovery that promotes emotional wellbeing, social connection and creative expression. Our approach incorporates integrated creative expression as instruments for self-healing and exploring identity.

We combine professional mental health care, SHIPS peer-led support, and community practices to provide inclusive and comprehensive mental health care.

We position ourselves as a unique blend of scientific evidence-based art therapy, creative fusion and a community-centred approach. Our multi-disciplinary programs adapt music, theatre, movement, and dance expression.

We educate individuals to use art as a healing tool to self-regulate their mental health. It provides a unique and beautiful technique to encourage individuals to release and process their emotions, offering an accessible alternative to traditional therapy.

Absolutely not. Our programs focus entirely on expression, release, and process, not on talent or skill. The goal is internal transformation, not a finished artwork.

We employ a multi-modal and integrated creative expression therapy approach tailored to the program’s goal. This includes painting, collage, spontaneous movement, creative writing, music, all designed to give you several pathways for emotional discovery and processing.
