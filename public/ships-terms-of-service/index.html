SHIPS Terms of Service.

Last updated: 27 April 2026

Welcome to SHIPS by myWIPhealing. Before you access our services, please read these Terms of Service. Your use of Social Healing Impact Peer Support (SHIPS):

By signing up, you are entering a shared space grounded in peer support, psychological safety, and collective responsibility. Your participation reflects informed consent and a commitment to uphold the following principles.

1. Stewardship and Framework

All SHIPS materials, content, and methodologies are part of a stewarded healing framework by
myWIPhealing, including all intellectual property (IP). By participating, you agree:

  • To access and use SHIPS content only within authorised roles and spaces.
  • Not to reproduce, adapt, distribute, or deliver SHIPS materials externally without formal
    approval.
  • To respect that all frameworks and practices are community-informed and ethically governed.
  • This stewardship protects the sustainability and integrity of SHIPS as a healing ecosystem.

2. Ethical Use & Boundaries Practice

SHIPS operates as a peer support model, not a clinical or medical service. By participating, you agree:

  • Not diagnose, treat, or provide medical or psychological advice.
  • Practice within the role of peer support, not as a professional authority.
  • Encourage referral to qualified professionals when appropriate.
  • Use SHIPS identity only when formally recognised.
  • Not position SHIPS as a replacement for clinical care, but as a complementary support space.
  • Clear boundaries ensure safety for both participants and the community.

3. Confidentiality & Psychological Safety

SHIPS spaces are built on trust, consent, and emotional safety. By participating, you agree:

  • To protect personal stories and lived experiences shared within SHIPS spaces.
  • To practice consent-based sharing at all times.
  • To respect group confidentiality in both online and offline settings.
  • To obtain explicit consent before sharing any content externally, including on social media.
  • Psychological safety is a shared responsibility upheld through confidentiality and respect.

4. Cultural Integrity & Trauma Awareness

SHIPS is grounded in Nusantara values and trauma-informed principles, recognising the diversity
of participants’ lived experiences. By participating, you agree:

  • Engage with respectful, non-judgmental, and compassionate communication.
  • Practice cultural humility across differences in identity, belief, and background.
  • Be mindful of trauma and hold space with sensitivity and care.
  • Honour shared responsibility in maintaining inclusive and respectful environments.
  • Healing spaces are sustained through mutual respect across differences.

5. Ethical Data & Reflective Research Practice

SHIPS may engage in learning, reflection, and research-informed practices to improve community
impact. By participating, you agree:

  • That anonymised insights or reflections may be used to strengthen SHIPS practices and
    programmes.
  • That no personal identifying information will be shared without explicit, informed consent.
  • To respect internal materials, processes, and community knowledge as protected and non-
    extractive.
  • To uphold ethical participation in any reflective or research-related activities.
  • This ensures that knowledge is developed responsibly, without compromising individual safety
    or dignity.

6. Protection of Community Integrity

SHIPS is sustained through collective care, accountability, and ethical participation. By participating, you agree:

  • To uphold SHIPS values in your participation and interactions
  • Not to misuse SHIPS spaces, identity, or community for personal, commercial, or harmful
    purposes
  • To respect facilitators’ roles in maintaining safety and community wellbeing
  • That participation may be reviewed or discontinued if agreements are not upheld
  • Protecting the community ensures the continuity of a safe and trusted healing space.

Acknowledgement & Consent

By proceeding with registration, you confirm that:

  • You have read and understood these Terms of Service.
  • You agree to participate with respect, responsibility, and consent awareness.
  • You understand the non-clinical nature of SHIPS.
  • You commit to upholding the shared values that sustain this community.
