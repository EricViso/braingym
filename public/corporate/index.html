Well-being as risk mitigation strategy.

Sustained performance depends on engaged, resilient employees. When wellbeing is unsupported, risks surface across productivity, culture, and cost.

Employees are present but disengaged

Employees who are “present but absent” cost money. Our Mental Agility protocols use art therapy to restore focus, engagement, & problem-solving energy.

Burnout is high & ongoing

Long-term stress increases errors and health risks; driving hidden operational costs. Our programs help employees recognise and regulate stress before it impacts health and work.

Retaining talent is challenging

Trust and safety is essential for workforce growth. We build and empower peer leadership practice that strengthens team connection & long-term employee retention.

Frequently Asked Questions.

myWIPhealing stands for ‘my work-in-progress healing’. We are a Malaysian social enterprise dedicated to holistic recovery that promotes emotional wellbeing, social connection and creative expression. Our approach incorporates integrated creative expression as instruments for self-healing and exploring identity.

We combine professional mental health care, SHIPS peer-led support, and community practices to provide inclusive and comprehensive mental health care.

See comparison.

Conventional trainings like Employee Assistance Programs (EAPs) typically provide confidential counselling and crisis support for employees experiencing personal or mental health challenges. These services are essential in helping individuals navigate difficult situations.

myWIPHealing focuses on creative expression and emotional regulation for workforce wellbeing. Our programs help employees build emotional resilience, communication skills, and stress management capabilities through experiential creative engagement and psychology education.

Rather than replacing EAP services, our programs are designed to complement existing wellbeing infrastructure that can work alongside EAP services, leadership development programs, and employee engagement initiatives.

We have packages tailored to every organization’s needs. Please use the Request Proposal button to receive a detailed breakdown tailored to your organization’s size and specific needs.

Our metrics are measured using validated pre/post-program assessment tools. We provide your HR teams with anonymized, aggregate data and clinical insights that directly tracks improvements in key corporate KPIs.

Our onboarding begins with a structured operational assessment to identify workforce needs and potential risk areas. Based on these insights, we design a tailored programme with clear objectives and measurable outcomes.

We align with leadership through internal briefings to ensure strategic integration. Employees are then onboarded with clarity to encourage engagement and psychological safety.

Structured sessions and engagement activities are implemented with ongoing monitoring. We conclude with impact reporting and recommendations to sustain organisational wellbeing and performance.
