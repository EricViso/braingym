WIP HARMONI CIRCLE

Community care start with lived experiences

Monthly peer-led circles combining creative skills, lived experience, and collective healing. Hosted by our trained SHIPS peer supporters in a safe, community-first space.

Join our CircleMembers: upcoming Circles → Who are SHIPS?

More than just a hobby group

Life can feel isolating when we lack a third space, a place that isn’t work and isn’t home, but where we can simply *be*.
 
WIP Harmoni Circle is that space.

Rooted in Nusantara wisdom and collective healing, it’s a circle where you show up, express yourself and connect with others who get it.

The harmoni approach

Art jams & creative hangouts

Ethically guided, low-pressure art sessions designed to spark conversation and self-expression.

Different curated & immersive themes

Creative activity with materials provided. No skill needed, just your presence and a little spark of willingness & curiosity.

Peer-led centric Dialogue

Share your thoughts in a supportive, non-judgmental environment with SHIPS peers who also understand your struggles.

Clinical safeguarding

While the space is peer-led, a qualified mental health professional is always ensuring safety & providing support if needed.
