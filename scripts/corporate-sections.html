Our method · Creative expression therapy

When words run out, creativity speaks.

Few employees will say “I’m burning out” in a team meeting. But they can draw it, shape it, write it or move it. myWIPhealing’s creative expression therapy uses guided art, writing, movement and sound, led by mental health professionals, to help people name what they’re carrying, settle it, and turn it into skills they bring back to work.

  • Express

    Say what’s hard to say

    Art gives stress a shape, so it can be spotted and talked about before it turns into burnout, conflict or a resignation letter.

  • Regulate

    Calm the nervous system

    Hands-on, sensory activities quiet the stress response and restore focus, with micro-practices employees can use at their desks.

  • Understand

    Turn insight into evidence

    Every module captures short, validated self-assessments, so your organisation can measure wellbeing instead of guessing at it.

No artistic talent needed. Engineers, sales teams and senior leaders take part on equal footing, because the focus is the person, not the picture.
